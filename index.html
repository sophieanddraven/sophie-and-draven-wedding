OUR STORY

From Five Guys to forever.

Sometimes the best love stories start in the most ordinary places. Ours started at Five Guys in Great Falls, Montana.

Draven was working at the fryer, and Sophie thought he was cute. A friend offered to give her his Snapchat, and somehow that little moment turned into something much bigger.

Our story began on October 2, 2021. Since then, we've grown together, made countless memories, and built a life full of laughter, adventure, and love.

And this is only the beginning.
