A LITTLE BIT OF FATE

Our Story

♥

From Five Guys to forever.

Every love story has a beginning, and ours started in Great Falls, Montana, somewhere we never expected.

It all began at Five Guys Burgers and Fries in Great Falls. Draven was working behind the counter, making French fries at the fryer, when I walked in as a customer with my friend.

I spotted him and immediately turned to my friend and said, “Oh my gosh, that boy is really cute!” Luckily for me, she knew him and offered to give me his Snapchat.

I added him, he added me back, and before long, we were messaging and getting to know each other. What I didn't know at first was that Draven had noticed me, too. He thought I was really pretty, and those first sparks turned out to be mutual.

What started with a cute boy making fries and one little Snapchat add became the beginning of something so much bigger than we could have imagined.

Since October 2 2021, we've celebrated five years together, made so many memories, and grown together through different chapters of life. Now, we're getting ready for our next adventure: forever.

Who knew a trip to Five Guys could change everything?

Our forever starts here ♥
