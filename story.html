WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

From Five Guys to forever.

Some of the best things in life happen when you least expect them.

Every love story has a beginning, and ours started in Great Falls, Montana, somewhere we never expected.

When I walked into Five Guys with my friend, I noticed Draven behind the fryer. I thought he was cute, and somehow, that tiny moment became the beginning of everything.

A friend offered to give him my Snapchat, and from there, our story began. On October 2, 2021, we officially became us.

Who knew a trip to Five Guys could change everything?
