Getting Here

Great Falls is located in central Montana. If you're flying, Great Falls International Airport (GTF) is the closest airport.

If you're driving, please keep an eye on road conditions, especially during the winter months.

Winter in Montana

December in Montana can be beautiful, snowy, and very cold!

We recommend bringing warm layers, a winter coat, and shoes that can handle snow or ice. Give yourself a little extra travel time, too.
