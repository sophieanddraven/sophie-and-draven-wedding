Meet Us in Montana

Great Falls is our hometown, and we couldn't imagine a better place to celebrate our wedding.

Whether you're coming from nearby or traveling across the country, we're so grateful you're making the trip to celebrate with us!

✈

Getting Here

Great Falls is located in central Montana. If you're flying, Great Falls International Airport (GTF) is the closest airport.

If you're driving, please keep an eye on road conditions, especially during the winter months.

✧

Getting Around

Renting a car may be helpful if you're flying into Great Falls, especially if you'd like to explore the area.

Some of our favorite places are a little outside town, so having a vehicle can make your visit more flexible.

❄

Winter in Montana

December in Montana can be beautiful, snowy, and very cold!

We recommend bringing warm layers, a winter coat, and shoes that can handle snow or ice. Give yourself a little extra travel time, too.

A Special Place to Us

Showdown Montana

Located in the Little Belt Mountains near Neihart, Showdown holds a special place in our hearts. We ski here every year, and it's one of our favorite places to spend a Montana winter.

If you're up for a mountain adventure, we'd love for you to experience this place we love so much!
